# Rolls
## Ingredients
- 1 Cup of Whole Milk warmed to 110 degrees
- 2.25 teaspoons of yeast
- 1 tablespoon sugar (for activation)
- 1 tablespoon sugar 
- 1 egg
- .25 unsalted butter (melted)
- 3 cups of flour

## Steps
1. Combine warmed milk, 1 tablespoon of sugar, yeast into a bowl and let sit for 5 minutes
2. Add remaining sugar, egg, butter, salt and 1 cup of flour and combine.
3. Scrape bowl back down
4. Add additional 2 cups and knead until smooth
5. Let rise for 1-2 hours in a covered, greased bowl.
6. Portion and lay out in a greased pan
7. Let rise again for 30 minutes.
8. Bake at 350 w/ a tray of water for 25 minutes. Rotate the pans at 12:30.
